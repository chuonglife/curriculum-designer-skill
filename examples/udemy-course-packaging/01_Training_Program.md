# TRAINING PROGRAM: PACKAGING AND PUBLISHING AN ONLINE COURSE ON UDEMY

*(Structured after Appendix I, Circular 06/2022/TT-BLDTBXH, the national pedagogical training standard for vocational teachers in Vietnam)*

## I. TARGET LEARNERS

Teachers, lecturers, tutors, corporate trainers, coaches and mentors who want to turn what they already teach into a complete video course and publish it themselves on Udemy.

Learners need a computer with Internet access and basic word processing and presentation skills. No experience in video production or online selling is required.

## II. PROGRAM OBJECTIVES

#### 1. General objective

Equip learners with the competencies needed to design and produce digital learning materials, package a course, and publish an online course that passes Udemy's quality review, based on a sound digital instructional design process and the regulations that apply to selling digital content.

#### 2. Specific objectives

- Knowledge:
  - Describe digital teaching and learning, the main online learning models, and where a Udemy course sits among them;
  - Describe the online course design process and the structure of a digital course;
  - State the design principles for text, image, audio and video learning materials and the structure of a lecture video;
  - Describe the Udemy upload process, its review requirements, and the rules on copyright, personal data and tax that apply to selling online courses.
- Skills:
  - Write learning objectives with Bloom's taxonomy and select and structure digital content for a topic in their own field;
  - Design a lecture slide deck using a Slide Master and presentation design principles;
  - Record and edit lecture videos with interaction points that meet Udemy's audio and video quality requirements;
  - Write a teaching script, package and test a complete course;
  - Upload, configure and submit the course for review on the Udemy Instructor Dashboard.
- Autonomy and responsibility:
  - Plan and complete their own course product on schedule;
  - Comply with copyright, platform terms and the law when publishing and selling digital content.

## III. PROGRAM DURATION AND TIME UNITS

1. Program duration: 45 hours.

2. Time units: one theory hour is 45 minutes; one practice, integrated or discussion hour is 60 minutes; one assessment hour is 60 minutes.

### MODULE M02: DEFINING OBJECTIVES AND CONTENT FOR A DIGITAL COURSE

**Duration:** 6 hours (Theory: 3 hours; Practice, discussion: 2 hours; Assessment: 1 hour)

#### 1. POSITION AND NATURE OF THE MODULE

- Position: Delivered after M01 and before the materials design modules.
- Nature: A required module that integrates knowledge of learning objectives with the skill of structuring content. Learners leave with an objectives and content design that drives all later production.

#### 2. MODULE OBJECTIVES

On completing this module, learners are able to:

- Knowledge:
  - Describe Bloom's taxonomy (2001 revision) and the action verbs of each level;
  - Describe the characteristics of digital teaching content and the steps for defining it.
- Skills:
  - Write objectives for the course and for each lesson using the correct Bloom action verbs;
  - Collect, select, structure and refine the digital teaching content of their own course.
- Autonomy and responsibility: Review and revise their own objectives and content in response to feedback.

#### 3. MODULE CONTENT

#### 3.1. Content overview and time allocation

| No. | Lessons in the module | Total | Theory | Practice/ Discussion | Assessment |

|  | **Total** | **06** | **03** | **02** | **01** |

#### 3.2. Detailed content

**Lesson 1: Writing digital learning objectives with Bloom's taxonomy** - *Time: 03 hours*

\* Objectives:

On completing this lesson, learners are able to:

- Knowledge: State the 6 cognitive levels of Bloom's 2001 taxonomy and the action verb group of each.
- Skills: Write at least 3 objectives for the course and 1 for each lesson, each starting with a measurable action verb at the intended level.
- Autonomy and responsibility: Take care to remove unmeasurable verbs (understand, know, grasp) from their objectives.

\* Content:

1\. Bloom's taxonomy: concept and cognitive levels (2001 revision)  
2\. Writing learning objectives with the action verbs of each level  
3\. Practice: write course objectives and lesson objectives for the chosen topic.  

**Lesson 2: Defining digital teaching content** - *Time: 02 hours*

\* Objectives:

On completing this lesson, learners are able to:

- Knowledge: Explain partial and full virtualization of face-to-face teaching and the steps for defining digital teaching content.
- Skills: Build a content table for the course following the 4 steps (collect, select, structure, refine), with every content item tied to an objective.
- Autonomy and responsibility: Cut content that does not serve an objective, on their own judgment.

\* Content:

1\. Characteristics of digital teaching content: partial and full virtualization of face-to-face teaching  
2\. Steps for defining digital teaching content: collect, select, structure, refine  
3\. Practice: build the content table for your own course.  

#### 4. CONDITIONS FOR DELIVERING THE MODULE

4.1. Learning space: Self-paced online video.

4.2. Equipment: Computer, Internet.

4.3. Learning materials, tools and supplies: Bloom verb table (download), objectives table template, digital content table template.

4.4. Other resources: The course structure map produced in M01.

#### 5. ASSESSMENT CONTENT AND METHODS

5.1. Content

- Knowledge: Bloom's taxonomy and action verbs; the steps for defining digital teaching content.
- Skills: The objectives table and the content table of the learner's course.
- Autonomy and responsibility: Self-revision after feedback.

5.2. Assessment method: End-of-module assessment combining an objective test on Bloom's taxonomy with a submitted assignment (objectives and content tables) scored with a checklist.

#### 6. MODULE IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES

6.1. Scope of application: For all learners of the program.

6.2. Teaching and learning methods

- Instructor: Show badly written and well written objectives taken from real education courses; provide the verb table; have learners score each other's objectives.
- Learners: Watch the videos, take the quiz, write and submit the objectives table and the content table for their course.

6.3. Key points to emphasize

- Every objective opens with a measurable action verb;
- Every content item traces back to an objective.

#### 7. REFERENCES

[1] Anderson, L. W., Krathwohl, D. R. (2001), A Taxonomy for Learning, Teaching, and Assessing: A Revision of Bloom's Taxonomy of Educational Objectives, Longman.

[2] Ministry of Labour, Invalids and Social Affairs of Vietnam (2022), Circular 06/2022/TT-BLDTBXH, module on digital teaching in vocational education.

## VI. PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES

#### 1. Admission, delivery, assessment and certification

1.1. Admission

- Admit learners who match section I. There is no cap on numbers because the program runs online.
- Learners who already have a published Udemy course may be exempted from M05 and M06 on presenting evidence.

1.2. Delivery

- The program is delivered fully online as a self-paced video course on Udemy, with Q&A and submitted assignments.
- Lesson time is video watching time (theory) plus time spent following along and doing assignments (practice, discussion). Learners set their own pace. Completion within 6 weeks is recommended.
- The provider needs a Udemy instructor account, a way to manage submissions and a feedback turnaround of 7 days.

1.3. Assessment and certification

- Learners are assessed on a module once they have watched at least 80% of its videos and submitted all its assignments. End-of-module assessments are scored on a 10-point scale. Below 5.0 the product is resubmitted.
- The final program score is the rubric score of the complete Udemy course (M08). The other modules are prerequisites.
- Learners who complete 100% of the content receive Udemy's certificate of completion. Learners with a rubric score of 5.0 or higher also receive the provider's certificate, graded Excellent 9.0 to 10, Very good 8.0 to under 9.0, Good 7.0 to under 8.0, Pass 5.0 to under 7.0.

1.4. Recognition of prior learning

- Learners who have completed a recognized digital teaching module in a teacher training program may be exempted from M01 and M02.

#### 2. Requirements for the training provider

- The instructor has published at least one Udemy course that passed review and has teaching qualifications.
- All materials, templates, checklists and rubrics named in the modules are available. Screen recordings are updated when the Udemy interface changes.
- The provider uses this program as the basis for writing or selecting materials suited to online delivery.
