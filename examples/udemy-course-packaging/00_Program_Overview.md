# TRAINING PROGRAM OVERVIEW

## TRAINING PROGRAM: PACKAGING AND PUBLISHING AN ONLINE COURSE ON UDEMY

```
Program: PACKAGING AND PUBLISHING AN ONLINE COURSE ON UDEMY (45 hours)
|-- M01 - Overview of online course design and packaging (5 hours: Theory 3, Practice 2, Assessment 0)
|   |-- Lesson 1: Digital teaching and online courses (2 hours)
|   |   |-- Digital teaching and learning
|   |   |-- Why digital teaching is needed and what influences it
|   |   |-- Blended, hybrid, flipped classroom, online and distance learning
|   |   `-- Where a Udemy course sits in the online learning model
|   `-- Lesson 2: The online course design process (3 hours)
|       |-- The online course design process
|       |-- Structure of a teaching plan: objectives, activities, assessment
|       `-- Structure of a digital course: information, instructions, materials, interaction, assessment
|-- M02 - Defining objectives and content for a digital course (6 hours: Theory 3, Practice 2, Assessment 1)
|   |-- Lesson 1: Writing digital learning objectives with Bloom's taxonomy (3 hours)
|   |   |-- Bloom's taxonomy: concept and cognitive levels (2001 revision)
|   |   `-- Writing learning objectives with the action verbs of each level
|   `-- Lesson 2: Defining digital teaching content (2 hours)
|       |-- Characteristics of digital teaching content: partial and full virtualization of face-to-face teaching
|       `-- Steps for defining digital teaching content: collect, select, structure, refine
|-- M03 - Designing text and image learning materials (8 hours: Theory 3, Practice 4, Assessment 1)
|   |-- Lesson 1: Concept, formats and interaction in text and image learning materials (3 hours)
|   |   |-- Concept and characteristics of text and image learning materials
|   |   |-- Formats: slide decks, digitized books and documents, lecture notes, worksheets
|   |   `-- Interaction types: hyperlinks, navigation menus, acting on content (fill in the blank, multiple choice)
|   |-- Lesson 2: Presentation design principles and the Slide Master (2 hours)
|   |   |-- Presentation design principles
|   |   `-- Procedure for creating a Slide Master
|   `-- Lesson 3: Practice: designing lecture slides (2 hours)
|       |-- Step 1: design the topic slide
|       |-- Step 2: design the objectives slide
|       |-- Step 3: design the outline slide
|       |-- Step 4: design the content development slides
|       `-- Step 5: design the review, check and closing slides
|-- M04 - Designing audio and video learning materials (8 hours: Theory 3, Practice 4, Assessment 1)
|   |-- Lesson 1: Concept, formats and interaction in audio and video learning materials (3 hours)
|   |   |-- Concept and characteristics of audio and video learning materials
|   |   |-- Production formats: slides converted to video; images converted to video; camera or screen recording
|   |   `-- Interaction types: questions inserted between segments; control menus; combining materials into a chain of learning activities
|   |-- Lesson 2: Design principles and structure of a lecture video (2 hours)
|   |   |-- Video design principles
|   |   `-- Structure of a lecture video
|   `-- Lesson 3: Practice: recording and editing a lecture video (2 hours)
|       |-- Preparing to record: lighting, sound, framing, checking slides and script
|       `-- Recording from the script; editing: cutting, inserting slides and images, inserting interaction points
|-- M05 - Writing the teaching script and packaging the complete course (9 hours: Theory 3, Practice 5, Assessment 1)
|   |-- Lesson 1: Writing the teaching script (3 hours)
|   |   `-- Structure of a teaching script
|   |-- Lesson 2: Packaging the complete course (3 hours)
|   |   `-- Components of a course package
|   `-- Lesson 3: Testing and correcting the course before publication (2 hours)
|       |-- Joining the lessons into one complete course
|       |-- Testing: take each lesson as a learner, check links, find display and audio errors
|       `-- Correcting from the test results; preparing the package for upload (M06)
|-- M06 - Uploading and publishing the course on Udemy (7 hours: Theory 3, Practice 3, Assessment 1)
|   |-- Lesson 1: The upload process on the Instructor Dashboard (4 hours)
|   |   |-- Creating the course: language, category and subcategory
|   |   |-- Filling in the course landing page
|   |   |-- Uploading the course image and promo video in the formats Udemy requires
|   |   |-- Building the curriculum: sections and lectures, uploading the videos and attachments packaged in M05
|   |   |-- Adding review questions or a quiz to each section
|   |   `-- Setting the price and the distribution programs to join
|   `-- Lesson 2: Quality requirements, review, and follow-up after publication (2 hours)
|       |-- Submit for review: Udemy checks audio, video and course structure before allowing publication
|       |-- Quality requirements: clear, uninterrupted audio without noise; sharp picture; truthful title and description
|       |-- Free preview lectures that let learners sample before buying
|       `-- Tracking reviews after publication to improve the course
|-- M07 - Legal rules for packaging, publishing and selling online courses (2 hours: Theory 2, Practice 0, Assessment 0)
|   `-- Lesson 1: Copyright, platform terms, data protection and tax (2 hours)
|       |-- Copyright and intellectual property in digital content
|       |-- Terms and policies of the Udemy platform
|       |-- Protecting learners' personal data
|       `-- Tax obligations
`-- M08 - Final program assessment (rubric for the complete Udemy course)
```
