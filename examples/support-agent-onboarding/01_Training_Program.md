# TRAINING PROGRAM: NEW SUPPORT AGENT ONBOARDING (WEEK ONE)

## I. TARGET LEARNERS

New customer support agents at a SaaS company; about 40 people in cohorts.

Entry: basic computer skills and written English; no support experience required.

## II. PROGRAM OBJECTIVES

#### 1. General objective

Equip new agents to handle customer tickets independently by the end of the first week, within the SLA and the quality rubric.

#### 2. Specific objectives

- Knowledge:
  - Describe the ticket life cycle, SLA, product and escalation rules;
- Skills:
  - Resolve, answer and escalate tickets in the helpdesk to the quality rubric;
- Autonomy and responsibility:
  - Take responsibility for their own queue and ask for help at the right time.

## III. PROGRAM DURATION AND TIME UNITS

1. Program duration: 24 hours.

2. Time units: one theory hour is 45 minutes; one practice, integrated or discussion hour is 60 minutes; one assessment hour is 60 minutes.

## V. DETAILED SYLLABUS

### MODULE M01: SUPPORT FOUNDATIONS AND THE TICKET WORKFLOW

**Duration:** 5 hours (Theory: 2 hours; Practice, discussion: 3 hours; Assessment: 0 hours)

#### 1. POSITION AND NATURE OF THE MODULE

- Position: First module of the program. It comes before all ticket-handling modules.
- Nature: Required module that integrates knowledge and skill; builds the ability to run one ticket through the helpdesk.

#### 2. MODULE OBJECTIVES

On completing this module, learners are able to:

- Knowledge: Describe the support team structure, service levels (SLA) and ticket life cycle;
- Skills: Process a ticket from queue to closure in the helpdesk tool within the SLA;
- Autonomy and responsibility: Comply with the team's SLA and tagging rules on every ticket.

#### 3. MODULE CONTENT

#### 3.1. Content overview and time allocation

| No. | Lessons in the module | Total | Theory | Practice/ Discussion | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lesson 1: Support team, SLA and ticket life cycle | 02 | 01 | 01 |  |
| 2 | Lesson 2: Working the queue in the helpdesk tool | 03 | 01 | 02 |  |
|  | **Total** | **05** | **02** | **03** |  |

#### 3.2. Detailed content

**Lesson 1: Support team, SLA and ticket life cycle** - *Time: 02 hours*

\* Objectives:

On completing this lesson, learners are able to:

- Knowledge: List the ticket statuses and the SLA target of each priority level without notes.
- Skills: Set priority and status on 5 sandbox tickets, all correct against the SLA table.
- Autonomy and responsibility: Comply with the SLA table when setting priority on every ticket.

\* Content:

1\. Team structure and tiers  
2\. SLA and priority levels  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;2.1. First response and resolution targets  
3\. Practice: set status and priority on 5 sandbox tickets  

**Lesson 2: Working the queue in the helpdesk tool** - *Time: 03 hours*

\* Objectives:

On completing this lesson, learners are able to:

- Knowledge: Describe the filters, views, tags and macros used in the helpdesk tool.
- Skills: Work 8 sandbox tickets from queue to closure in 90 minutes, with correct tags and at least one macro each.
- Autonomy and responsibility: Take initiative to flag a mis-tagged ticket to the team lead.

\* Content:

1\. Queue views and filters  
2\. Tags, macros and internal notes  
3\. Practice: clear an 8-ticket sandbox queue  

#### 4. CONDITIONS FOR DELIVERING THE MODULE

4.1. Learning space: Live online classroom (video conference) with breakout rooms.

4.2. Equipment: Laptop with webcam and headset, stable Internet, access to the training helpdesk sandbox.

4.3. Learning materials, tools and supplies: Sandbox ticket queue, macro library, scenario cards, scoring rubric.

4.4. Other resources: A senior agent as co-facilitator and the internal knowledge base.

#### 5. ASSESSMENT CONTENT AND METHODS

5.1. Content

- Knowledge: SLA levels and ticket statuses.
- Skills: Ticket queue worked in the sandbox.
- Autonomy and responsibility: Compliance with SLA and tagging rules.

5.2. Assessment method: Formative: facilitator checks the sandbox queue against a checklist.

#### 6. MODULE IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES

6.1. Scope of application: For all new support agents in the onboarding cohort.

6.2. Teaching and learning methods

- Instructor: Demonstrate live in the sandbox, then run timed practice rounds in breakout rooms and give feedback against the rubric.
- Learners: Join the live session with camera on, work tickets in the sandbox, review a peer's replies.

6.3. Key points to emphasize

- Every reply is checked against the rubric before it is sent;
- Learners work real-style tickets, not slides.

#### 7. REFERENCES

[1] Internal support playbook and knowledge base of the company (current version).

## VI. PROGRAM IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES

#### 1. Admission, delivery, assessment and certification

1.1. Admission

- Admit all new support hires; cohorts of up to 20 per live group.

1.2. Delivery

- Mostly live online sessions over five days, with a sandbox helpdesk.

1.3. Assessment and certification

- Learners attend at least 80% of hours; each module is scored 10-point scale; 7.0 needed to join the live queue.

#### 2. Requirements for the training provider

- Facilitator with 1 year of support experience.
- Sandbox helpdesk available for each learner.
