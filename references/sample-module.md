MODULE M02
TRAINING PROGRAM DEVELOPMENT
Duration: 40 hours (Theory: 13 hours; Practice, discussion: 25 hours; Assessment: 02 hours)

1. POSITION AND NATURE OF THE MODULE
- Position: A module in the pedagogical training program for teachers at college and intermediate vocational level, delivered after module M01.
- Nature: A required module that integrates professional knowledge, practical skills and professional attitude, giving learners the competence to design, evaluate and improve training programs.

2. MODULE OBJECTIVES
On completing this module, learners are able to:
- Knowledge: Analyze the methods, process and core content of designing, evaluating and improving the quality of a training program.
- Skills:
+ Produce an occupational analysis that fully describes the duties and tasks of an occupation or job position at college or intermediate level;
+ Write learning outcomes for the analyzed occupation that state specifically what learners can do after training;
+ Compile a training program for the chosen occupation in line with current regulations.
- Autonomy and responsibility: Carry out the plan for designing, evaluating and improving a training program for an occupation so that it meets the needs of the world of work.

3.2. Detailed content

Lesson 1: Methods and process of training program development - Time: 02 hours
* Objectives:
On completing this lesson, learners are able to:
- Knowledge: Explain the nature of a training program and the types of training program used in vocational education.
- Skills: Identify the method for developing a training program; analyze the content of each step of the development process.
- Autonomy and responsibility: Take the initiative in choosing the right method and process for a given program development assignment.
* Content:
1. General issues in training program development
1.1. Basic concepts
1.2. Types of training program in vocational education
2. Approaches, methods and process of vocational program development
2.1. Approaches to program development
2.2. The DACUM method
2.3. The vocational program development process
3. Discussion: Methods and process of vocational program development.

Lesson 3: Designing a training program - Time: 16 hours
* Objectives:
On completing this lesson, learners are able to:
- Knowledge: Describe the structure of a training program.
- Skills: Determine the objectives and learning outcomes, and design the program framework for an occupation at college or intermediate level in line with current regulations.
- Autonomy and responsibility: Carry out the assigned program design plan as scheduled.
* Content:
1. Designing objectives and learning outcomes
2. Designing the structure and content of the program
2.1. Program framework
2.2. Proposed teaching plan
2.3. Module and subject matrix
2.4. Content of modules and subjects
3. Determining resources and implementation guidelines
3.1. Teaching staff
3.2. Facilities and equipment
3.3. Implementation guidelines
4. Practice: Design the objectives and learning outcomes of a program; design its structure and content; determine resources and implementation guidelines.

(Lessons 2, 4 and 5 follow the same pattern.)

4. CONDITIONS FOR DELIVERING THE MODULE
4.1. Learning space: A well-lit classroom with room for discussion, group work and demonstration.
4.2. Equipment: Computer, Internet, printer, projector.
4.3. Learning materials, tools and supplies: Program development materials, A4 paper; current programs and teaching materials for the occupation.
4.4. Other resources: The detailed program and materials of the pedagogical training course.

5. ASSESSMENT CONTENT AND METHODS
5.1. Content
- Knowledge: Theory of training programs and program development; objectives and learning outcomes; the steps for determining them; the program development process.
- Skills: Designing, evaluating and updating a training program.
- Autonomy and responsibility: The degree to which the learner carries out the plan for designing, evaluating and updating a program for an occupation.
5.2. Method: Learning results are assessed through an end-of-module test in one of these forms: essay, oral exam, objective test, reflective report.

6. MODULE IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES
6.1. Scope of application: A module of the pedagogical training program for people who need to meet the pedagogical standard for teachers at college and intermediate level.
6.2. Teaching and learning methods
- Instructor: Set concrete exercises tied to real program development work; teach the theory, guide learners through the materials, run discussions, coach the design work; use active methods and interleave exercises, discussion and practice with theory.
- Learners: Attend, contribute, study the materials, work in groups or alone on each concrete task of program development, and report their products.
6.3. Key points to emphasize: Determining the objectives and learning outcomes of a program; designing, evaluating and improving a program.
